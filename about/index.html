Who We Are

Supporting the infrastructure that keeps business moving.

Laist Panoptic operates in the transformer and electrical power equipment sector, supporting commercial and industrial clients with equipment supply, refurbishment, maintenance and technical services.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the requirement, identify the right solution and provide the technical support needed to keep critical electrical infrastructure operating reliably.


How We Work

Understand. Solve. Deliver.

Every electrical requirement is different. We take the time to understand the application, operating environment and technical requirements before recommending a solution.

Whether the requirement involves new equipment, an existing transformer, refurbishment or ongoing maintenance, our focus remains the same: provide a practical solution that supports reliable operation.
